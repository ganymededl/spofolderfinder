SharePoint Folder Finder

Built by David S. Liu for SLED • July 17, 2026

Reads the folder path out of the link, strips the tracking parameters, and rebuilds proper SharePoint Forms/AllItems.aspx?id=... folder-view URLs that actually open in the browser — so you never have to URL-decode by hand or click down through nested folders again.

The green row is the folder that houses the video (where your sibling assets live); the grey Library tag marks the top-level document library.

Paste any SharePoint / Stream video link — the messy Outlook one or the “Copy link” one — and get clean, clickable links to the folder that holds it (and every folder above it).

Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Tip: it also parses the moment you paste.
